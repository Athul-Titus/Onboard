# Engineering Handbook

> **Sample Data**: This document is synthetic sample documentation created for OnboardOS multi-agent orchestrator evaluation and demonstration.

## Welcome to Engineering
Welcome to our engineering team! We build resilient, high-scale software that empowers our customers worldwide. Our engineering culture values high ownership, psychological safety, continuous learning, and clear documentation.

## Communication Norms
- **Slack**: Used for synchronous chat and quick questions. Use public channels (e.g. `#eng-general`, `#ask-backend`, `#ask-frontend`) over direct messages to keep knowledge searchable for everyone.
- **Async First**: Document technical decisions in RFCs or Notion pages before scheduling meetings. Respect team members working across multiple time zones.
- **Office Hours**: Every tech lead holds weekly office hours for design discussions, pairing sessions, and onboarding questions.

## Daily Rituals
- **Daily Standup**: Most teams run a 15-minute async or hybrid standup between 9:00 AM and 10:30 AM local time.
- **Sprint Cadence**: We operate on 2-week sprints starting every other Wednesday. Sprint retrospectives occur on the closing Tuesday.
- **Demo Days**: Alternate Fridays at 4:00 PM are reserved for company-wide engineering demos celebrating shipped work.
