# Engineering Handbook

## Welcome to Engineering
Welcome to the engineering organization! We build resilient, high-scale software that empowers our customers. Our engineering culture values high ownership, psychological safety, continuous learning, and clear documentation.

## Communication Norms
- **Slack**: Used for synchronous chat and quick questions. Use public channels (e.g. #eng-general, #ask-backend) over DMs to keep knowledge searchable.
- **Async First**: Document decisions in RFCs or Notion pages before scheduling meetings. Respect team members across time zones.
- **Office Hours**: Every lead holds weekly office hours for design discussions and onboarding questions.

## Daily Rituals
- **Daily Standup**: Most teams run a 15-minute async or hybrid standup between 10:00 AM and 10:30 AM local time.
- **Sprint Cadence**: We operate on 2-week sprints starting every other Wednesday. Sprint retrospectives occur on the closing Tuesday.
- **Demo Days**: Alternate Fridays at 4:00 PM are reserved for company-wide engineering demos.
